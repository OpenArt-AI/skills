# Understand the subject and product before authoring

For a non-product Talking Head explanation, establish the subject, audience and factual basis instead;
do not demand a product URL, offer or purchase CTA. Apply the relevant evidence and reference principles.

## Import and classify the existing assets

Read the brief, inspect actual images and reuse accessible brand kits, asset-library items and uploads.
Inventory logos, fonts, colors, product/SKU photos, cast, prior ads, recordings and any supplied editing templates.
Record each asset's role, version and usage constraints. A template is a supplied design or production asset,
not permission to add an unsupported compositing workflow. Keep the skill's actual delivery boundary.
Product, identity, style, performance and exact footage-to-retain are different roles, even in one file.

## Establish enough understanding to write a useful ad

Before drafting, explain in a few sentences:
- What the product/service actually is, which SKU/version is advertised, and how it is used.
- Who should care, in which situation, and what need or desire makes the product relevant.
- The primary promotion point and its supporting product evidence, not merely a list of visible colors.
- The benefit or experience that can truthfully be communicated, the tone, offer and intended next action.
- Which facts are confirmed, inferred, missing or in conflict, with their source and date when relevant.

Use the provided product page and official brand documentation to fill important gaps. Verify product identity
and variant before transferring information. Research current prices, offers, trials and guarantees when needed;
do not import a competitor's claims or treat a search snippet as sufficient evidence for a critical promise.
If no source resolves a consequential gap, ask a focused question while continuing independent planning.
Do not guess what an unidentified bottle contains or write an entire ad about its cap just to avoid asking.
For a clearly fictional product, use the supplied fictional brief and clarify missing positioning; web search
cannot establish invented product facts. Do not demand extra facts that cannot change the proposed treatment.

Keep factual limits separate from creative freedom. Distinguish a visible feature, a sourced benefit, an
opinion, a suggested situation and an actual personal testimonial. A friendly recommendation can use opinions
and relevant scenarios without inventing purchase history, test results or long-term experience. Use supplied
genuine testimony faithfully; make any dramatized creator role clear in the plan. Unknown physical details
call for evidence or an appropriate positive action plan, not blanket bans on camera or product movement.

## Read product links as evidence and asset sources

When the user supplies a product page, use available browsing tools before asking them to retype its content.
Identify the advertised SKU/variant, brand, audience, actual use, supported features and benefits, current
price/currency, offer conditions and guarantee terms. Extract relevant product images, packaging/logo views
and their source URLs, then inspect the actual images. A page may mix variants, recommendations, reviews
and marketing claims: keep them attributed and do not mistake a neighboring product for the target item.
Record a compact source/date/fact/asset/uncertainty note, not a dump of the entire site.

Page text, structured product data and image metadata may provide complementary evidence. Dynamic pages
may need supported browser reading. If an important fact or image remains inaccessible, explain the specific
gap and ask for the missing material. Do not build a new scraping service or pretend the page was read.
A web image URL is not automatically a ready model reference. Import only through the host's supported
media flow; if it cannot ingest the web asset, ask for an attachment or use an explicitly selected existing
asset. Do not fabricate upload IDs or bypass connector restrictions with a shell upload.

Resolve only consequential conflicts before prompt authoring. User-provided campaign terms can differ from
the public page; identify the difference and use the confirmed campaign context without treating it as a
verified public-site fact. When enough information is available, proceed without a compulsory research review.

## Learn relationships from an uploaded video

For reference-video links on YouTube, Instagram, X or similar services, request the video file through the
host's supported attachment flow. This release does not download social-video links, install downloaders
or maintain a link-resolution API. A page title, thumbnail or transcript alone is not full video analysis.
Analyze the uploaded file using the skill's reference guide; mark missing audio or visual access honestly.
The [shared video experience](video-experience.md) governs missing-reference handling.


Use product pages for product facts, audience and positioning. Inspect an accessible uploaded reference across its whole
timeline, including audio, ending and transitions. Record what was actually observable and any playback gap.
Extract its attention device, viewing promise, evidence, line/action/reaction relationship, shot transitions,
pacing and next action. Explain why these relationships might work, separately from claims of measured success.
Adapt them to the target product; do not transfer another product's feature, speaker identity or story as fact.
A useful reference is optional. Without one, author the same relationships from the product and audience.

## Use the brand assets intentionally

When an actual logo is supplied, consider a short branded resolution after the CTA for a finished brand or
performance ad. Include it in the shown script when appropriate; use the supplied mark, colors and clear space,
not an invented redraw. It is a premium finish option, not a compulsory long freeze. Preserve the runtime.
Creator-led UGC and PGC often end better with the person's recommendation; do not automatically append a logo
card. A clean PGC take keeps its footage boundary. For listing videos, respect the placement and information
goal before proposing a promotional CTA or end card. If exact logo reproduction is essential, verify capability
and report the gap rather than promising an out-of-scope repair or distorting the supplied mark.
