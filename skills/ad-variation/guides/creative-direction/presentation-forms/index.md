# Presentation forms are relationships

Use these readings to identify what carries attention and meaning. They are compatible with one another and do not select a preset composition. An unfamiliar reference may need a different arrangement; describe it and build it.

| Dominant relationship | Starting reading |
| --- | --- |
| A person addresses the viewer; performance carries the argument | [Direct address](direct-address.md) |
| Evidence or a demonstration alternates with or shares space with the presenter | [Explanation and demonstration](explanation-and-demonstration.md) |
| Turns, reactions, or a physical situation carry the meaning | [Conversation and enacted scenes](conversation-and-scenes.md) |

Determine what persists across the piece, what changes at each passage, and why. A format name is less useful than knowing whether the product demonstration interrupts a claim, proves it, or is the main action being narrated. Use the technique pages for the resulting material and design decisions.
