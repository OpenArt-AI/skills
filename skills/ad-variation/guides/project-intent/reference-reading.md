# Read the reference as a designed experience

Start with the entire reference, including the ending and its sound. Probe duration, dimensions, frame rate, audio streams, and rotation metadata. Preserve the measured duration in the brief; never infer it from an attachment thumbnail or a model default.

Make an initial whole-piece account: the opening promise, progression, evidence or payoff, speaker/listener relationship, and ending. Then inspect the picture and speech together. A transcript is useful evidence but cannot explain a cut, expression, interface change, or visual joke by itself.

Build time-labeled frame grids at useful scales. A sparse grid locates scenes; denser grids around cuts and transformations explain motion. Place spoken phrases beside the corresponding ranges. Inspect the surrounding frames when the beginning or final state could mislead. Watch the clip with sound to understand delivery, pauses, and transitions. Use [capability selection](../execution/capability-selection.md) for available probing, extraction, and transcription capabilities.

Record observations such as: "The comparison remains visible while the speaker gives both numbers; the verdict appears on the last word." Then separately interpret: "The persistent comparison reduces memory load, and the late verdict supplies the payoff." This gives the new work a usable relationship instead of a list of visual ingredients.

For each coherent visual behavior, identify:

- What enters, what remains, what changes, and what leaves.
- Which words, gestures, beats, or authored events cause those changes.
- Whether elements share one layout/motion system or are independent overlays.
- Framing, crop, readable scale, hierarchy, typography, color, texture, and sound relationships.
- Which details recur, and where repetition meaningfully changes.

Do not assume every overlay was made by a model, every scene change is a cut, or every person is a separate layer. A flattened video is evidence of the final image, not access to its original project. Recover the intended relationship and implement it with the available tools.

Maintain coverage of the whole source. Inspect every distinct behavior and all important transitions, then deepen uncertain passages. Save frame timestamps and relevant crops so another reviewer can trace your conclusions. When direct audio/video playback is unavailable, say what was actually inspected and keep auditory acceptance unresolved.

For a remake, translate this reading into a treatment and a keep/change map using [adaptation](adaptation.md). For source editing, also retain frame-accurate source ranges. The output should be an understanding the Agent can compose from, not a demand to replay a reference layout unchanged regardless of the new script.
