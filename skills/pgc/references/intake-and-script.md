# Intake, reference roles and speech

Apply [intent and script development](intent-and-script-development.md) before classifying supplied text.
A detailed script or timed plan is a draft unless exact execution is explicitly requested or it is already
approved and requested unchanged. Develop the intended message in this skill's voice before plan review.

Identify the presenter's role and the viewing promise: product reviewer, subject explainer, educator or
brand spokesperson. Establish the camera position, deliberate lighting, background, on-camera product use
and requested degree of formality. A home location can still be PGC when its filming setup is intentional.
Do not infer expertise, credentials, independent testing or actual ownership from a polished presentation.
If a request is primarily an informal selfie or lived-in daily product-sharing moment, identify it as UGC;
honor an explicitly chosen PGC treatment and explain any material mismatch without silently changing style.

Read the request, assets and prior decisions first. Record only what changes generation: message/audience,
presenter, language, framing/aspect ratio, setting, wardrobe, speech length, locked dialogue, chosen model,
authorized generation scope and reference roles. Separate target speech length, source-media length,
per-request model limits and measured generated duration.

| Input role | Use | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity/performance recording | Face, natural expression, gesture and delivery when supported | Exact voice cloning or permission to reuse its script |
| Identity photos | Appearance and visible wardrobe | Vocal identity or temporal performance |
| Creative reference | Hook, rhetorical progression, pacing, framing, energy | Target identity, verified product claims, or a requirement to reproduce its overlays |
| Product evidence | Visible appearance and documented capabilities | Unseen features, performance numbers, reviews or personal experience |
| Approved copy | Exact spoken content and requested pronunciation | Permission to shorten locked wording |

For a real presenter, prefer an existing clear speaking recording, then a suitable new recording if needed,
then real photos when recording is impractical. Inspect useful ranges and live reference limits before selecting
the upload. Do not force a new recording if existing assets suffice. If no real identity is requested, direct
a fictional presenter. Keep voice identity separate from appearance and delivery style.

Create a keep/change map for revisions: words, face, vocal qualities, wardrobe, background, framing, gesture,
pace and tone. Lock what the user requested, not every accidental detail in the reference. A new performance
does not provide strict source editing or pixel preservation.

When writing or improving supplied draft copy, choose one clear message grounded in provided or verified facts. Record a compact
claim/evidence/uncertainty ledger. Do not invent endorsements, buying experiences, prices or results.
Only visible product details can be inferred from an image alone. Unconfirmed product claims need clarification
or removal from newly authored copy; flag conflicts in locked copy instead of silently changing it.

Write for natural speech with manageable clauses, breathing room, and a plausible point of view.
A hook, explanation and next action may help, but are not a required script template. Do not insert a CTA
unless it fits the request. Keep stage directions and pronunciation hints separate from spoken words.
Estimate duration to plan requests; only the returned recording establishes actual timing.

All essential meaning must work through the presenter and speech. A reference whose payoff depends on a UI
reveal or B-roll needs an adapted spoken explanation within the user's scope; if that changes the intended
claim or locked wording, surface the conflict. Do not invent visual proof or generate supporting shots.

Persist the brief, script and clip plan. Apply [generation approval](generation-approval.md):
prepare full prompts and matching timed plan rows before review, including exact speech and all planned text;
use the shared Draft/full-quality defaults and show clip counts and the proposed ratio in the same decision.
Reuse unchanged choices explicitly approved for this scope.

## Develop sparse and detailed drafts through useful reasoning

Before authoring, read [recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md) and
[latte reference script](recommendation-examples.md). Connect the listener's actual situation to supported details,
meaningful operations and reasons to choose or understand. Natural speech needs a developing thought, not
just shorter sentences and a friendly tone. For educational work, use the same reasoning without forcing a sale.
Keep all essential meaning in speech and the same presenter shot. Commercial facts should fit the explanation;
required on-screen cards conflict with this clean-footage output and must be resolved explicitly.
