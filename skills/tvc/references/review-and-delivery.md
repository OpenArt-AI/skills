# Review the commercial, not just its first frame

Review the whole returned video with sound, then inspect difficult passages at higher detail. Measure actual
duration, dimensions and streams; use decoding/frame inspection where available to locate freezes, black
ranges or missing media. A deliberate pause, dark image or silent moment is not automatically a defect.

Compare the result to the brief, product ledger, treatment and selected references:
- Completeness: all essential beats, required copy and the intended ending fit the requested delivery.
- Brand idea: the visual world and progression communicate the chosen impression rather than generic spectacle.
- Product presence: recognizable shape, readable scale and a meaningful connection across the film.
- Fidelity: correct SKU, geometry, color, labels, logo, cast, wardrobe and physical object state.
- Photography: purposeful camera behavior, credible material/light, composition and continuity.
- Closing image: a clear visual resolution with the product/brand attention required by the brief.
- Typography: accurate wording and brand form, readable duration and no unwanted graphic additions.
- Sound: intended music/ambience, intelligible and complete speech, lip sync where relevant, no distortion.
- Music: the planned score is actually audible, expresses the brand idea, develops with the film and resolves
  with its ending; verify deliberate unscored passages too. Use [music and sound](music-and-sound.md).
- Technical result: usable video, expected aspect ratio and no unintended missing or repeated sections.

Inspect exact brand letters and labels at useful scale, assisted by OCR if available; OCR does not verify
wordmark shape, product accuracy or tracking. Listen to audio rather than inferring its quality from a stream
or transcript. Record timestamped findings as accepted, rejected or unverified, with evidence and limitations.

A correction targets a specific cause in direction, references or settings. Preserve the previous result and
valid assets, then review the entire regenerated request for collateral changes. Do not call a new full-video
generation an exact local repair without evidence. Stop repeated unresolved retries within the execution rules.

Deliver the selected video through an accessible file/link or supported preview, plus its prompt, references,
settings/task receipt and concise review record. Label alternatives and rejected/unverified candidates clearly.
Do not promise an editable timeline, exact deterministic rebuild, broadcast certification or measured audience
performance. Prompt-only delivery is complete only when that was the user's request.

An unresolved product, typography, sound or preservation requirement remains visible in the handoff.
Unassembled clips must be labeled as clips, not the requested complete commercial.

## Creative intent check

Play the whole result and record timestamped findings: does it develop as planned, does the person
react credibly to what they say, does product action add useful information, and does an ending hold
serve an actual purpose? A fixed camera can pass. Unmotivated stillness, repetitive posing or stiff
delivery cannot be accepted merely because fidelity or technical checks pass. Prompt compliance
does not establish video quality. Mark these checks unverified when playback is unavailable.
