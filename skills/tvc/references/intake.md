# Intake for a brand commercial

Read the brief, supplied assets and prior decisions before questioning the user. Establish the intended brand
impression and deliverable first. Do not assume a premium tone, an actor, a story, voiceover or a text end card.

Identify the task:
- New concept from sparse product material: inspect facts and choose a justified direction.
- Detailed brief or draft script: retain its intended brand idea and required information; develop stronger visual progression, writing and sound.
- Explicitly locked or approved unchanged script: execute the settled version; surface any real conflict.
- Reference adaptation: state which visual/rhetorical relationships transfer and what must change.
- Existing output correction: inspect the actual defect and the previous prompt/receipt before changing it.
- Variants or format/language changes: identify variables and dependencies while preserving settled choices.
- Prompt-only or analysis: produce the requested material without paid generation.

A useful brief records objective, audience, intended impression, product/SKU, key message, brand assets,
distribution/aspect ratio, duration, speech/text languages, sound needs, and locked or excluded elements.
Infer low-impact choices from supplied material; ask only about gaps that affect product truth, the creative
direction, essential fidelity or authorized execution. Do not make every field a blocking questionnaire.

Classify assets as product evidence, packaging/logo, identity/wardrobe, setting, style, camera/motion, sound,
approved copy, or source footage to edit. One source may have several explicit roles.
A brand guideline constrains design; a competitor reference does not supply product facts.

Recommend duration from the brand idea, script and meaningful action, not an automatic 30-second slot.
Recommend a ratio for the intended placement and obtain approval if it was unspecified. Verify the live
model before committing to request counts; source duration, final duration and request limits differ.

Summarize the commission and a proportional treatment: brand idea, product role, visual relationships,
sequence progression, sound direction and intended ending. When the brief already settles these, proceed.
When an important ambiguity changes the concept or a locked requirement, expose that specific decision.

For an adaptation, write a keep/change map covering product, world, cast, language, rhythm, graphics, sound and
ending. A fresh brand film can transfer a relationship without copying the original composition sequence,
distinctive slogan or identity.
