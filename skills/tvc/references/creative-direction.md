# Design a brand idea through image and sound

Start with the impression the audience should retain and a product-specific reason for that impression.
Choose a small set of visual elements and relationships: shape, scale, texture, color, motion, space or behavior.
The product belongs among these elements from the beginning.

If the product could be replaced by an unrelated category without changing the treatment, make the concept
more specific. "Premium" or "cinematic" cannot substitute for composition, light, material and sound decisions.

## Choose the expressive form

Select or combine forms according to the brief, without forcing a menu or fixed story:
- Product/sensory: make verified material, mechanism, texture or sound carry the experience.
- Motion/scale: organize the complete product, space, speed and camera relationship.
- Human/editorial: use posture, clothing, action and environment as part of the composition.
- Life/narrative: use a concise human situation when it advances the brand idea.
- Conceptual: develop a coherent metaphor or visual rule tied to the product.

An actor need not speak or enact a problem-solution story. A playful everyday treatment can be a complete
brand commercial. A premium treatment can be bright, forceful or funny; slow motion, black/gold, shallow focus
and expensive architecture are not universal defaults.

## Make the photography concrete

For each key image decide subject position and size, orientation, foreground/background relationships,
negative space, light-dark balance, color emphasis and the source of motion.
Allow purposeful asymmetry, depth, occlusion and scale changes. Do not make every image centered with a blurred
background or treat changing locations as sufficient shot design.

Camera direction includes its starting relation, path, speed changes, parallax/occlusion and ending relation.
A move should reveal, compare, energize or resolve something. Avoid repeating push-ins, arbitrary orbits or
focus pulls that repeatedly make the product unreadable. The subject, fabric, light or environment may move
while the camera stays still.

Specify light direction, source size/hardness, shadow, separation and the material event it reveals.
Retain believable glass thickness, metal reflections, fabric structure, weight and contact. Surreal space can
coexist with credible material behavior; it should not falsely demonstrate an unverified product function.

## Organize progression and product presence

Choose an opening, development and resolution appropriate to the concept, not mandatory chapter lengths.
Every important cut should change a meaningful scale, viewpoint, action, rhythm, color or emotional relation.
Hold key images long enough to recognize the product. Quick cuts should not conceal its shape.

Establish product recognition or a clear product connection early, then sustain it through action or motifs.
A delayed reveal is valid when the brief supports it and earlier material makes the reveal meaningful.
Do not spend most of the film on unrelated scenery before introducing a new object at the end.

## Design sound and closure

Normally develop a brand-appropriate score using [music and sound](music-and-sound.md).
Choose sonic texture or a motif, its entry, progression, contrast and resolution. Make space for product,
material and environmental sounds. Silence may carry a beat. Do not default to piano/strings, music on every
moment, or a whoosh on every cut. Speech must remain intelligible when present.

Design a final image that completes this particular idea: product arrangement, a readable moving product,
or a product-led composition within the established world. Resolve visual motifs, motion and sound.
Budget enough time for recognition and any requested reading; do not append the ending beyond the runtime.

Brand name and slogan are choices governed by the brief and assets, not automatic additions because a logo
is visible on packaging. If text is required, use exact approved wording, an accurate brand reference,
readable hierarchy and a purposeful position. A no-added-text film can close through product, image and sound.

## Turn discovery into a memorable product experience

Link establishing context, a meaningful material/action detail and the resolved product impression.
For example, move from noticing an object in a real setting to tactile interaction, then a viewpoint
that makes its distinguishing form memorable. Each transition should add information or feeling.
A close-up or focus change earns its place by revealing a specific relationship, not by appearing
cinematic. Transfer this logic from references rather than their unrelated prop or exact shot count.
When supplied brand assets and the brief suit it, propose CTA followed by a brief accurate logo
resolution under [product understanding](product-understanding.md), within the planned runtime.
