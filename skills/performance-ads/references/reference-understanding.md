# Read a reference as a complete experience

Read the whole source before extracting a style. Measure duration, dimensions, frame rate, rotation and audio
streams with available media tools. Review the opening, progression, evidence, payoff and ending with sound.
Use time-labeled frame grids for coverage, denser samples around changes, and transcripts alongside the picture.
A still cannot establish motion; a transcript cannot establish vocal delivery, sound design or lip sync.

For each meaningful passage, record:
- What the viewer sees, hears and understands, and when those states change.
- Subject and product position, scale, framing, hierarchy, readable areas and occlusion.
- Camera path, subject motion, light, materials, color and recurring visual motifs.
- Words, gestures, cuts, transitions, graphic entrances/exits and their triggers.
- Product evidence, emotional progression, and the relationship between the opening and ending.

Separate observation from interpretation. "The label stays visible while the hand rotates the lid" is evidence;
"this makes the mechanism easy to understand" is an interpretation. Preserve useful relationships instead of
merely listing ingredients. A flattened video does not reveal whether a graphic was generated or composited,
whether a scene change was a cut, or which original layers existed.

Classify each source's role: product truth, identity, wardrobe, setting, layout, visual style, movement, sound,
or the actual source to edit. A creative reference does not establish the target product's features or the
target person's identity. A source used for analysis need not be submitted to the generation model.

For an adaptation, write a keep/change map and explain how the relationship transfers to the new product and
message. Different words usually require different timing. Do not force the old timestamps onto new speech
or transplant a reference's claims, slogans or unrelated props. For an exact replacement, record the required
source duration, sound, framing and untouched regions, then verify whether the selected model supports that
contract. A fresh generation is not proof of source preservation.

Save source IDs, inspected ranges, relevant frame timestamps, observations and unresolved questions.
If audio, playback, an image or a source URL cannot be accessed, say exactly what was inspected. Do not claim
full audiovisual understanding from contact sheets. Continue usable analysis while identifying material gaps.
No specific transcription engine, Hypit project or storyboard generation is required for reference analysis.

## Type-specific reading

Locate the opening attraction, moment of product recognition, reason to continue, evidence and payoff. Separate attention, comprehension and action. Inspect the role of speech, captions, feature copy and decorative graphics independently.
