# Review attention, understanding and execution separately

Review the complete output with sound, plus a silent pass when the brief or placement requires silent
comprehension. Inspect metadata and technical usability; then compare the actual video to the brief,
product ledger and intended sequence. A strong first frame is not evidence of a successful ad.

Record timestamped findings for:
- Opening: the event is understandable and connected to the product or promised payoff.
- Progression: the viewer has a reason to continue, and the expected payoff occurs.
- Communication: one main point is clear, with the needed visual or verbal evidence.
- Fidelity: product/SKU, labels, identity, hands, props and actions are accurate and consistent.
- Graphics: requested layers appear, unwanted layers do not, wording is exact and information is readable.
- Offer/action: prices, discounts, links, CTA wording and urgency match supplied or verified facts.
- Sound: complete intelligible speech, credible lip sync, intentional music/effects and no unwanted audio.
- Music: planned BGM is audible, fits the treatment, leaves speech/product sounds clear and finishes coherently;
  deliberate unscored passages remain unscored. Use [music and sound](music-and-sound.md).
- Ending: the action, message, joke, loop or CTA resolves as intended.
- Technical delivery: correct aspect ratio, usable duration, streams and no unintended freezes or missing ranges.

Use OCR when available to assist exact-text comparison, then inspect context, placement and tracking.
Transcription can assist speech completeness but does not establish delivery quality or lip synchronization.
A technically valid task can still fail its product, message or creative requirements.

For controlled variants, compare the intended constants in actual results. Note when body footage, lighting,
voice, product detail or timing changed beyond the chosen variable. Do not describe a comparison as isolated
when the outputs introduce meaningful confounds. Evaluate conceptual differences as hypotheses, not measured
conversion improvements.

If a defect is localized inside a single generated ad, revise the responsible direction/reference/settings
and inspect the entire regenerated result. Preserve the prior accepted assets and receipt.
Do not silently remove required copy, mute essential speech, or hide geometry errors with decoration.

Deliver accessible selected videos, variant labels, the complete prompts, task/settings/reference records,
and accepted/rejected/unverified review findings. Explain limitations requiring unavailable playback or manual
review. Do not claim measured performance, universal platform compliance or an editable post-production project.
If only prompts were requested, deliver them with the assumptions and variant rationale without implying that
their video quality has been tested.

## Creative intent check

Play the whole result and record timestamped findings: does it develop as planned, does the person
react credibly to what they say, does product action add useful information, and does an ending hold
serve an actual purpose? A fixed camera can pass. Unmotivated stillness, repetitive posing or stiff
delivery cannot be accepted merely because fidelity or technical checks pass. Prompt compliance
does not establish video quality. Mark these checks unverified when playback is unavailable.
