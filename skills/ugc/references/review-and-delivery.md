# Review credibility, handling and the actual media

Watch the whole video with sound. Probe duration, aspect ratio, streams and complete frame coverage.
Inspect difficult moments around reaching, opening, turning, changing grip, glancing and finishing sentences.
Stills, transcripts and an audio stream alone do not establish natural delivery or lip sync.

Record timestamped evidence and accepted, rejected or unverified status for:
- Creator: consistent face, hair, wardrobe, body and natural skin through movement.
- UGC treatment: believable everyday situation and phone framing without imposed studio presentation,
  gratuitous shake, constant grinning, exaggerated shock or announcer speech.
- Words and voice: exact approved dialogue, language, pronunciation, natural pace, pauses and complete endings.
- Lip sync and sound: matching mouth movement, intelligible native speech and appropriate ambience/music.
- Music: planned BGM is actually audible, fits the product and performance, stays behind speech and ends
  coherently; deliberate unscored choices remain unscored. Use [music and sound](music-and-sound.md).
- Product: accurate SKU, proportions, materials, hardware, logo and supported visible details.
- Handling: plausible contact, hands, camera holder, object count and continuous open/closed states.
- Evidence: no invented ownership, test result, endorsement or unsupported visual performance claim.
- Sequence: useful product visibility, coherent transitions and completed operation/ending.
- Text: requested captions or labels spelled correctly, legible and timed; no unrequested graphics.
- Delivery: agreed format and complete video, or explicitly agreed raw clips with correct speech coverage.

Compare variants for unintended changes to creator, voice, product, setting and message. A nominally isolated
hook change can alter the whole model output. Preserve accepted versions and identify the failed request before
regenerating within scope; do not hide defects with invented post-production or missing required content.

Return selected files or accessible links, version/clip order, exact dialogue mapping, prompts, model/settings,
reference/task records and concise QA. For a clip set, label it unassembled. For unavailable playback, name the
unchecked dimensions. A convincing simulation is not a verified customer testimonial or conversion improvement.

## Creative intent check

Play the whole result and record timestamped findings: does it develop as planned, does the person
react credibly to what they say, does product action add useful information, and does an ending hold
serve an actual purpose? A fixed camera can pass. Unmotivated stillness, repetitive posing or stiff
delivery cannot be accepted merely because fidelity or technical checks pass. Prompt compliance
does not establish video quality. Mark these checks unverified when playback is unavailable.

## UGC advertising acceptance

For an ad authored from basic product information, separately judge early audience relevance,
relatability of the tailored creator/setting, specific reasons to recommend, meaningful demonstration,
delivery that follows the thought, speech-to-duration fit and a complete brief-appropriate ending. Quote timestamped
evidence. A technically correct but flat professional product recital is not a successful UGC treatment.
Check against the approved developed script; explicit exact-copy requirements and exclusions override defaults.

## Review the writing as its own deliverable

Before generation, inspect the exact speech without performance adjectives. Identify the specific listener
need, the supported detail, and why it matters in use. The recommendation should contain useful reasoning
even without its discount. Watch for interchangeable claims, a shuffleable feature list, fabricated life
history, decorative handling, and offer lines that replace the argument. More words and more excitement
do not fix missing substance. A supplied draft can be improved; only explicit locks or the approved unchanged version constrain rewriting.

After generation, check that useful spoken connections survived, that product actions match the explanation,
and that delivery varies with meaning rather than selling every line at the same intensity. Review exact
commercial wording and the ending too. Record timestamped evidence; leave voice and motion judgments
unverified when full playback is unavailable. Use [recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md).
