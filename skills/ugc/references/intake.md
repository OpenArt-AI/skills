# Intake for everyday creator videos

Apply [intent and script development](intent-and-script-development.md) before classifying supplied text.
A detailed script or timed plan is a draft unless exact execution is explicitly requested or it is already
approved and requested unchanged. Develop the intended message in this skill's voice before plan review.

Read existing material and decisions before asking questions. Identify new creation, approved-script execution,
reference adaptation, output correction, variations or prompt-only analysis. Ask only about consequential gaps.

Record the audience, one intended message, product/SKU, creator identity or fictional role, everyday situation,
spoken language, duration, aspect ratio, exact copy, visual/text/audio requirements and generation scope.
Clarify whether the user wants a complete model-generated video or independent raw takes only when ambiguous.
Default to one fictional adult creator if identity is unspecified; do not demand identity uploads unnecessarily.

| Material | Use | Do not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity photos | Appearance and visible wardrobe | Voice or lived experience |
| Speaking recording | Appearance, manner and supported vocal conditioning | Guaranteed voice cloning |
| Creative reference | Delivery, framing, rhythm and situation | Target identity or true product claims |
| Product views/specifications | Appearance and documented operations | Unseen construction or performance |
| Approved script | Exact words and requested pronunciation | Permission to shorten locked wording |
| Customer evidence | Specifically documented experience and authorized copy | A generic testimonial for another creator |

Choose a plausible camera arrangement: arm's-length selfie, phone propped nearby or another person holding it.
If both hands must operate the product, use a supported propped-phone or helper-camera setup. Do not describe
the creator as simultaneously holding the phone, opening a package and manipulating multiple accessories.

Identify whether the requested mode is everyday product sharing or a staged professional explanation.
A phone on a stable support can still be UGC; a home studio with a deliberate presentation can be PGC.
For mixed briefs, name the requested combination instead of silently enforcing a genre stereotype.

For sparse or detailed draft ad briefs, choose a product-specific recommender and develop a complete recommendation
with specific life reasons, meaningful product use and naturally integrated requirements. Read
[recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md) before drafting. Recommend duration from natural delivery and useful action. A vertical 9:16 treatment may suit
the placement; present the proposed ratio with the shared preview/full-quality choice under
[generation approval](generation-approval.md).
Dialogue follows the requested language; captions follow the brief. Preserve locked words and resolve timing conflicts.

Create a keep/change map covering words, creator, voice, wardrobe, location, handling and camera.
Show an agent-authored script and planned video/request counts, then ask whether to generate.
Reuse explicitly approved choices for the same scope without repeating the question.
