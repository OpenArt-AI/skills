# Direct a close, persuasive recommendation

## The defining relationship

The creator feels like a relatable person sharing a discovery with someone close: inviting, upbeat, emotionally
engaging and keen to explain why this product is worth considering. This recommendation energy is the first
creative priority. UGC is not a professional media presenter delivering a formal product assessment in a home.
Everyday surroundings alone are insufficient; point of view, proximity, expression and interaction create trust.
Keep realistic skin, believable voice and practical handling. Natural does not mean timid, flat or low-energy.

Read [recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md) before selecting performance adjectives.
Choose a reason to speak now: a recognizable frustration, an outfit decision, a delightful visible discovery
or a relevant task. Connect the hook to the product early and let the demonstration earn the recommendation.
Turn a feature into a useful or desirable consequence supported by evidence; do not merely read specifications.
Opinions and suggested situations can be vivid without fabricated personal history or results.

## Tailor the person and surroundings

When cast and setting are absent, propose a fictional adult creator whose style, voice, manner, wardrobe and
everyday environment fit the product, audience and promotion. State why this person would credibly share it.
Personality and distinctive styling can help; do not default every product to the same neutral presenter or
infer real credentials or experiences from appearance. Follow supplied identities and explicit casting choices.

| Product/context | A possible relationship and setting | Performance direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fashion/accessories | Style-conscious friend getting ready by a mirror or wardrobe | Playful confidence; try-on discovery, a delighted look, invitation to imagine the outfit |
| Cosmetics/skincare | Approachable beauty enthusiast at a lived-in vanity | Interested, tactile, inviting; show supported texture/use and a specific reason to care |
| Everyday household item | Resourceful person in a kitchen, laundry or daily task | Warm recognition of the annoyance, practical demonstration, satisfying relief |
| AI/software | Curious maker, student or working creator at a real desk | Brisk discovery and clear explanation of a verified workflow; share the useful result |

These are examples, not casting stereotypes or fixed layouts. Match the actual brand rather than copying
the category row. A distinctive character should remain approachable and relevant, not distract from the offer.

## Make enthusiasm observable

Direct the thought behind delivery: recognize the viewer's problem, lean into the useful discovery, look at
the demonstrated detail, react to what it reveals, then return to the viewer with a warm recommendation.
Connect selected phrases to actions: "look at this detail" brings it close; a styling suggestion changes the
relevant item; a gifting idea can land with a knowing smile. Do not perform identical gestures on every line.
Vary emphasis, pace and expression with meaning. A constant sales smile, forced astonishment or monotone list
does not communicate contagious enthusiasm. Use an inviting point of view, not a corporate announcer voice.

Choose close, readable phone-native framing and a plausible camera arrangement. A propped phone, helper
camera, handheld view or motivated detail cut can all work. Let the person and environment feel accessible.
Keep hands and phone ownership physically feasible. Design positive camera and product actions; never add
unrequested blanket motion bans to imitate authenticity or reduce generation risk.

## Finish the recommendation

For an ad from a sparse brief, organize the useful reasoning with
[ad structure and pacing](ad-structure-and-pacing.md). Finish with the required or appropriate next step, naturally spoken
by the creator. An offer or guarantee belongs only when supplied or verified. Develop detailed drafts as
well as sparse briefs under [intent and script development](intent-and-script-development.md). Explicit
verbatim requests, approved unchanged scripts and raw-footage/non-ad scope take priority over defaults.
Do not automatically replace the human finish with a detached logo card or long frozen pose.
