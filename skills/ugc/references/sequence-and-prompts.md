# Turn the treatment into executable direction

Create a proportional beat plan: time estimate, camera arrangement, action and object state, exact speech,
product evidence, sound and any text. Leave time to open the mouth, handle the object, pause and finish words.
Duration estimates guide planning; only the returned video establishes actual timing.

For every independently submitted prompt include:
1. Actual supported reference attachments and their roles; creator/product/wardrobe locks.
2. Everyday setting, available-light direction, phone position, crop and who holds the camera.
3. Ordered action beats and product states, including hand occupancy and any justified shot change.
4. Exact dialogue in the requested language, separated from acting and pronunciation notes.
5. Intention, natural pauses, gaze changes, pace, lead/tail room and supported duration.
6. Native speech, room tone and operation sounds, plus the chosen background music treatment or a deliberate
   unscored exception; follow [music and sound](music-and-sound.md) for balance, rhythm and ending.
7. Caption/text behavior, ending and brief-specific exclusions, such as no cuts for a raw take.

Use a complete coherent prompt, not disconnected fragments that omit the later action or ending. When the user
wants one paragraph, consolidate without dropping locks. Use clear sentences and avoid English em dashes.
Bind actual media labels returned by the tool; do not assume a written @image token attaches an image.

Example structure, adapted to actual evidence: a phone rests near a kitchen counter; the creator picks up the
referenced bag, opens the documented pocket, points to its visible opening and sets it down while explaining
that detail. The exact words come from the brief/evidence, not an invented purchase story or performance claim.

If captions are requested, specify exact copy, language, placement and approximate timing separately from speech.
Do not assume the model will spell or time them correctly, and do not promise later typography repair.
Generate an identity or scene still only when needed; inspect face, hands, product and framing before reuse.

## Show the script as an ad before converting it to model direction

First develop the speech with [recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md). Use
[ad structure and pacing](ad-structure-and-pacing.md) to organize the reasoning, required copy and ending.
In the user-facing script, pair each spoken beat with its visual evidence and performance intention.
Specify why this creator, location and tone suit the product; translate enthusiasm into changing attention,
meaningful emphasis and visible reaction. Show the full ending and count of proposed outputs.
A model prompt must retain those choices, not replace them with a neutral catalog description.
