# Product truth and creator credibility

Maintain a small evidence ledger: intended statement or demonstration, source, confirmed fact versus visual
inference, approved wording and unresolved gap. Inspect product images at useful scale before planning action.
Lock silhouette, proportions, color, material appearance, structure, hardware, logo, SKU and accessory count.
Keep identity, product, scene and style references separate.

Images support visible design observations, not waterproofing, battery life, medical outcomes, durability
or hidden specifications. A lifestyle scene must not imply an unverified result through imagery either.
Show supported angles and operations; avoid invented backs, interiors or mechanisms when source coverage is thin.

Do not invent purchases, long-term use, comparative tests, personal outcomes, reviews, credentials or endorsements.
A generated first-person performance is not real customer evidence. A scripted present-tense observation such as
"Here is the side pocket" can accompany a visible action without inventing "I've carried this for six months."
Use genuine supplied testimony only within its supported context; do not attribute it to an unrelated actor as fact.

For missing evidence, use a visible feature or a non-experiential explanation in new copy. If the conflict is in
locked text, surface it instead of silently rewriting. Prices, offers and guarantees require supplied or verified
current evidence when used; do not add them simply to make the ending more sales-oriented.

Respect specified identity and wardrobe while keeping natural skin texture. Photos do not establish voice.
An accurate portrait is not proof that a generated product or motion is also accurate; verify each separately.

## Keep useful life context when personal history is absent

Use [recommendation writing](recommendation-writing.md) to connect a supported operation or feature to a
recognizable task, a conditional choice or an authored preference. Explaining why a pocket's placement would
help someone reach a needed item is more useful than naming the pocket, without inventing months of ownership.
Skin feel, absorption, taste and efficacy still need appropriate evidence; neither packaging nor generated
performance verifies them. Preserve the distinction between an illustrative situation and actual testimony.
