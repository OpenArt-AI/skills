# Unscored sound for a consistent hook set

Disable background music throughout the base ad, every replacement hook and every assembled deliverable,
including Draft previews. Do not add a bed, song, rhythmic backing or musical CTA sting. State this in the
Script Plan and each prompt; use a supported music control if one exists, without inventing a field.
Keep speech enabled when required. This scoped policy does not change other advertising skills.

Use natural speech, useful product sounds and appropriate room ambience. Silence can be intentional.
Do not substitute a rhythmic effects track for prohibited BGM. Design pace through argument, delivery,
action and editing, without forcing rapid speech to compensate for missing music.

Keep voice character, acoustic space, pronunciation and level coherent between openings and the body.
Shared images alone do not lock vocal identity. Retain accepted body audio rather than regenerating it
for each version, and avoid doubling native audio during assembly.

Listen for unwanted music, clipped syllables, noise-floor changes and mismatched voices in each export.
A prompt saying no music is not proof of compliance. Fix a noncompliant opening within approved scope
or report it; never silently mute speech. See [assembly and seams](assembly-and-seams.md).
