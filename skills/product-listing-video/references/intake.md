# Intake for a product video

Inspect product references, specifications, brief and previous choices before asking questions.
Establish what the viewer needs to understand in the product-detail media gallery: product overview,
feature explanation, usage instruction, accessory overview or another stated listing context.
For Amazon or another named store, record the marketplace/locale, intended gallery slot if known, item/SKU,
existing companion images and whether a cover/thumbnail is requested. Do not require a listing URL when
supplied assets and a draft listing brief are sufficient. Do not promise control of platform display order.

Identify the task:
- Sparse product-only brief: choose a useful, evidence-supported presentation.
- Detailed draft or specification script: preserve required facts and improve the buying-question logic, demonstration and information order.
- Explicitly locked or approved unchanged plan/copy: preserve the settled version and develop execution.
- Reference adaptation: transfer presentation relationships without importing another product's facts.
- Existing result repair: inspect exact spelling, geometry, action or information defects.
- SKU/format/language variants: define what changes and which facts/assets belong to each version.
- Prompt-only or review: stop at the requested deliverable without paid generation.

Record SKU, color, size or variant identifiers; supplied views; visible packaging and labels; verified features,
dimensions, accessories and quantities; the intended information priority; exact required wording; duration,
aspect ratio, language, audio and graphic preferences. Do not require dimensions or accessories when irrelevant.

Separate a physical product from packaging, included accessories and scene props. A prop in a lifestyle reference
is not automatically part of the offer. A product family image is not permission to mix variants.
When only a front view is known, plan supported viewpoints instead of inventing the back for an orbit.

For sparse briefs, start with a concise product overview, approximately 10 seconds if the selected message fits.
Recommend aspect ratio from the stated placement and verified specifications; obtain approval when unspecified.
Do not presume a square image gallery
requires a square video. A white product setting with concise callouts is one useful approach, but actual
user intent may call for a clean showcase, real setting or operation.
Do not impose white backgrounds, captions or leader lines on a request that is better served without them.
Model feasibility is checked before committing; there is no universal skill duration maximum.

Choose the smallest set of product facts that meaningfully helps the viewer. A feature-heavy specification list
does not require every fact in a short video. Preserve locked copy; surface an actual timing conflict rather
than deleting a claim or speeding through unreadable labels.

If a marketplace or placement is named, use supplied requirements or verify relevant current specifications
when needed. Do not promise platform compliance based solely on a generic square format.
Distinguish video technical requirements, cover/thumbnail requirements and main-image rules. Verify only the
current requirements relevant to the requested asset; do not treat this skill as a marketplace policy source.
Summarize the intended information flow, product locks and required evidence in a proportional treatment.
