# Verify the product and information in the actual video

Watch the entire output and inspect difficult details at useful scale. Review with sound, plus silent viewing
when the intended commerce placement relies on visual comprehension. Measure duration, dimensions and streams;
inspect decoding/frame coverage for unintended freezes, missing scenes or black intervals.

Compare against the product ledger, brief and shot plan:
- Identity: exact SKU/color, silhouette, proportions, structure, hardware, packaging and logo placement.
- Geometry: no invented rear/inside features, warped openings, changing seams or impossible construction.
- Quantity/state: correct accessories, props, repeated objects and open/closed states.
- Operation: physically coherent hands, contact, hinges, closures, motion and completed outcome.
- Information: each selected feature is supported, understandable and not overstated by text or imagery.
- Text/numbers: exact model names, labels, dimensions, units and any approved offer.
- Annotations: correct anchor, readable hold, stable tracking, useful placement and no accidental occlusion.
- Presentation: accurate color/material, informative views and enough time for recognition.
- Sound: requested audio, intelligible complete speech when present, and no distracting or unwanted content.
- Music: planned BGM is audible, supports inspection without masking useful product sounds, and ends coherently;
  confirm intentional unscored sections. Use [music and sound](music-and-sound.md).
- Completion: the intended final product/state and correct technical delivery.
- Listing fit: the same item/SKU as the supplied companion images, clear product recognition and useful
  shopping information without an invented offer or off-site action.
- Placement: match verified video requirements and separately requested cover requirements; distinguish
  unchecked marketplace rules from checked media properties. Do not imply that the item has been uploaded,
  approved or assigned a specific gallery position by generating its assets.

Use OCR if available to compare requested copy, then inspect the actual letters, units, placement and tracking.
A near-match model number is a defect when an exact identifier is required.
OCR cannot verify mechanical accuracy or whether a pointer names the correct part.
Do not infer product correctness from a polished first frame.

Record timestamped accepted, rejected or unverified findings. If only images or metadata were inspectable,
leave motion/audio-dependent checks unresolved. A generated demonstration does not establish real-world
performance beyond supplied evidence.

For corrections, identify whether the source, claim, angle, prompt, reference binding or setting caused the
problem. Change the relevant request within authorized scope and review the entire returned video again.
Do not silently delete required labels or use decorative effects to conceal a malformed product.
No deferred post-production repair is assumed.

Deliver selected videos with clear SKU/version labels, complete prompts, reference/settings/task records,
and concise QA. Keep rejected and unverified candidates distinct from accepted results.
Do not call incomplete clips a finished listing video or claim marketplace approval, measured sales impact
or exact source preservation without supporting evidence.

## Creative intent check

Play the whole result and record timestamped findings: does it develop as planned, does the person
react credibly to what they say, does product action add useful information, and does an ending hold
serve an actual purpose? A fixed camera can pass. Unmotivated stillness, repetitive posing or stiff
delivery cannot be accepted merely because fidelity or technical checks pass. Prompt compliance
does not establish video quality. Mark these checks unverified when playback is unavailable.
